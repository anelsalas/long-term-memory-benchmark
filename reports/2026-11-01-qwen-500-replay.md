# Qwen replay with a 500-token reply allowance

November 1, 2026. Codex summary.

The local Qwen3-0.6B Q8_0 reader answered the original 20 correction histories
in the same four conditions. All 80 requests completed. The saved model ID,
prompt hashes, condition order, selected evidence and expected grades matched
the baseline. Every new reply continued the exact text of its earlier reply.

| Evidence supplied | Correct grade stated | Correction present | Abstained | Mixed | Exact integer match |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Question only | 0/20 | 0/20 | 20 | 0 | 0/20 |
| Full history | 20/20 | 20/20 | 0 | 0 | 0/20 |
| Keyword top one | 0/20 | 0/20 | 16 | 1 | 0/20 |
| Correction alone | 20/20 | 20/20 | 0 | 0 | 0/20 |

In the first case, Lina's original grade was 23 and her corrected grade was
82. With the full history or the correction alone, Qwen said her grade was 82.
With no history it said UNKNOWN. Keyword search supplied the original record,
and Qwen said no score was recorded.

The native grader retains the original integer-only rule. All 40 correct
answers in the full-history and direct-evidence conditions were sentences.
For the retrospective content reading, the reporter removes the timestamp and
person identifiers and reads an unambiguous grade or abstention before comparing
with the ledger. We inspected the raw replies. In the keyword condition three
answers stated an incorrect grade, and one combined an abstention with a
conditional grade. With no history, abstention was appropriate in every case.
These results cover 20 score variations of one development template.
